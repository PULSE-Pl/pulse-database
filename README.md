# P.U.L.S.E.
**Public Utilities & Linear Structures Engineering**

> *Open-source international platform for infrastructure engineers and enthusiasts exploring the industry.*

---

## 🎯 Mission & Vision
**P.U.L.S.E.** is a collaborative engineering knowledge base and open-source ecosystem dedicated to linear infrastructure: highways, urban street networks, bridges, railways, pipelines, and utility corridors. 

We bridge the gap between theoretical standards, practical CAD/BIM workflows, and modern engineering automation.

---

## 🧭 Content Tracks & Structure

Our repository is organized into three progressive tracks designed for different stages of professional growth and technical exploration:

| Track | Focus Area | Target Audience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01. Industry Overview** | Global infrastructure landscape, sector analysis, standards overview, and fundamentals | High school students, university applicants, enthusiasts, and anyone exploring the industry |
| **02. Career Guides** | Professional roadmaps, interview prep, relocation insights, and career milestones | University students and graduate engineers planning their growth |
| **03. Articles & Automation** | Open-source scripts, technical articles, CAD/BIM workflows, and R&D notes | Pcractcing engineers, developers, and researchers building technical solutions |
