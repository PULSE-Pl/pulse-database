# Contributing Guidelines & Platform Charter

Welcome to **P.U.L.S.E.** (*Public Utilities & Linear Structures Engineering*). To maintain an objective, high-signal international engineering knowledge base, all contributors and community members must strictly adhere to the core principles and technical rules outlined below.

---

## ⚖️ Core Principles (Non-Negotiable)

### 1. Zero Commercial Promotion & Zero Politics (Zero-Tolerance Policy)
* **Strict Neutrality:** Any form of advertising, hidden marketing, affiliate links, or promotion of commercial software, vendors, or paid courses is strictly prohibited. Mentioning specific CAD/BIM tools or standards is permitted **only** in an objective, technical, or comparative context.
* **Apolitical Environment:** Political statements, geopolitical debates, and ideological commentary are completely banned across articles, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
* **Enforcement:** Violations of this rule are subject to a **zero-tolerance policy** — offending content will be immediately removed without prior warning, and the responsible account will be permanently banned from the organization and its repositories.
* **Goal:** Deliver academically sound, unbiased, and universally accessible engineering knowledge.

### 2. Anti-Lobbying & Equal Opportunity
* **No Institutional Privilege:** No company, university, professional body, or commercial/non-profit organization holds special privileges or editorial control on this platform.
* **Zero Discrimination:** Discrimination, gatekeeping, or bias based on nationality, gender, age, current or past employer, academic background, or membership in any professional association is strictly forbidden. Contributions are evaluated solely on their technical merit.

### 3. The Decalinguo Principle
* **Global Accessibility:** All published articles are structured for translation (automated baseline with community-driven manual refinement) into **10 core languages**, ordered geographically from East to West (identified strictly by ISO 639-1 codes without national flags):
  1. `ja` — **Japanese** (日本語)
  2. `ko` — **Korean** (한국어)
  3. `zh` — **Chinese** (中文)
  4. `ru` — **Russian** (Русский)
  5. `ar` — **Arabic** (العربية)
  6. `de` — **German** (Deutsch)
  7. `fr` — **French** (Français)
  8. `en` — **English** (English)
  9. `es` — **Spanish** (Español)
  10. `pt` — **Portuguese** (Português)
* **Writing for Translation:** Authors should write clearly, avoid untranslatable idioms, and define regional engineering abbreviations upon first use so materials translate accurately across all ten languages.

---

## 🛠️ Technical & Editorial Standards

### 4. Engineering Rigor & Verifiability
* Technical claims, formulas, and design parameters must cite official design codes (e.g., AASHTO, UK DMRB, Dubai RTA, Eurocodes, GOST/SP) or verifiable engineering literature.
* Personal career experiences and regional industry insights (primarily in `02-career-guides/`) must be clearly distinguished from normative technical requirements.

### 5. Intellectual Property & Licensing
* Do **not** upload copyrighted full-text standards, proprietary company templates, or confidential project data.
* All submitted text, diagrams, and automation scripts (`03-articles-automation/`) must be original or properly attributed and compatible with the repository's **MIT License**.

---

## 📁 Submission Workflow

1. **Choose the Right Track:**
   * `01-industry-overview/` — Sector fundamentals, standards overviews, terminology.
   * `02-career-guides/` — Career roadmaps, interview preparation, relocation insights.
   * `03-articles-automation/` — Technical articles, CAD/BIM scripts, open-source tools.
2. **File Naming:** Use lowercase `kebab-case` (e.g., `geometric-design-standards-overview.md`).
3. **Pull Requests:** Fork the repository, create a descriptive branch, and submit a Pull Request with a clear summary of your contribution.
